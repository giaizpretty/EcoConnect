♻️ RECYCLE RIGHT

Recycling Guide

📦 Paper & Cardboard

Keep paper and cardboard clean and dry before recycling them.

🥤 Plastic

Check local recycling rules before placing plastic items in recycling bins.

🍾 Glass

Separate glass containers where recycling facilities accept them.

🔋 Batteries

Do not place batteries in ordinary bins. Use proper collection points.

💧 EVERY DROP COUNTS

Simple Ways to Save Water

🚿 Shorter Showers

Reduce the amount of water used while bathing.

🚰 Turn Off Taps

Don't leave the tap running when brushing your teeth.

🔧 Fix Leaks

Repair leaking taps and pipes to prevent unnecessary water loss.
